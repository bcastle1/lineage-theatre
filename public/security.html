Security at Lineage Theatre

Public registration creates a customer account with a personal password of 12–128 characters and explicit terms/privacy consent. Registration cannot grant an administrator role or claim a verified email address. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes in private Vercel Blob storage. Sessions use signed, secure, HTTP-only cookies with expiry. Changing a password invalidates older sessions, and suspended accounts cannot sign in or continue using a session. Existing temporary passwords must be changed before the studio can be used.

Family working projects and original source files are stored in your browser. Use a trusted device and keep independent copies. A separate, optional cloud archive stores selected finished films and limited film details in private Vercel Blob storage. Archive access is checked on the server for the film owner or an authorized administrator; it is not a public sharing link or an automatic backup of all your materials.

The service owner delegates administrator access through private, expiring invitations. Roles come from stored server records. Registration, sign-in, archive uploads, and administrative changes have request limits; app mutations check their origin. New account records are created without overwriting an existing account. Administrative changes are logged in private storage.

Provider credentials stay on the server. GPT-6 Astra story generation, MagicLight film generation, and QuickBooks charges and refunds remain pending verified connections. They do not activate when a credential or pricing setting is entered. The default 0% markup can be changed by an administrator for future quotes; confirmed provider costs and customer approval are still required before a charge.

Only the service owner can connect or disconnect the QuickBooks business authorization. The authorization return uses a short-lived, single-use browser verification and rechecks the owner's account. Saved Intuit tokens use authenticated encryption in private server storage and never appear in the browser or administrator listings. Connection status alone does not confirm merchant capabilities, payment capture, or refund completion.

Read our privacy information for the material stored locally, saved to the optional cloud archive, or supplied to a connected AI provider with your consent.

Report security concerns or request account help at admin@brocotech.ai. Do not include passwords or private family documents in a security report.
