Security at Lineage Theatre

Studio access requires an invited account and a personal password. Temporary passwords must be changed before the studio can be used. Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes in private server storage. Sessions use signed, HTTP-only cookies with expiry and are invalidated when a password changes.

Family projects and original source files are stored in your browser. They do not sync across devices and are not an encrypted cloud vault. Use a trusted device, sign out when finished, and keep copies of original sources and downloaded films.

Provider credentials stay on the server. Paid shot requests require authentication, are subject to request limits, and are tied to the signed-in account. Uncertain submissions are not automatically repeated. AI assistance and external studios process the material you choose to send, as described in our privacy information.

Report security concerns or request account help at admin@brocotech.ai. Do not include passwords or private family documents in a security report.
