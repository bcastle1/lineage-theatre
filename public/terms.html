Using Lineage Theatre

Lineage Theatre accepts public account registration for developing films from family photographs, records, memories, and recordings. Registration creates a customer account. You must be at least 18. Keep your password private, submit only materials you have authority to provide under the agreement you accept, and respect the privacy of living relatives.

Ownership of submitted sources

New registrations must separately accept the Source Materials Ownership and Use Agreement. It transfers the rights you own or are authorized to transfer in sources you subsequently submit to Lineage Theater, including documents and photos, and permits commercial reuse, publication, licensing and sharing with third-party companies. Read it before registering or submitting material. Your entered legal name and checked acceptance box serve as your electronic signature. You can print or save the agreement shown to you.

Possession of a file does not necessarily mean you own its copyright. The agreement cannot transfer someone else's rights or override mandatory privacy protections. It does not apply retroactively to earlier accounts or materials without separate acceptance. Later edits preserve the version previously accepted by each customer.

AI story suggestions and cinematic scenes are creative proposals. Review the historical evidence, names, dates, relationships, and any dramatization before creating or sharing a film. Generated content is not genealogical proof.

Working projects and original sources are saved in your browser. Keep your original materials and download project backups and finished films. You may separately opt to save film details and a selected finished video to the private cloud archive. That archive is accessible to you and authorized administrators. Private storage describes access controls; rights to reuse material are governed by the agreement you actually accept. The archive does not automatically back up your source documents or full working project. Clearing browser data does not remove your account or cloud archive.

Story development may send the selected narrative and sources to an AI service after the consent shown in the app. Film production and payment availability depend on the connected services and are shown in the studio. When checkout is available, payment processing is provided by Intuit Payments Inc. Preparing a price does not itself charge you or produce a film.

A film's price uses a verified production quote when available, or a planning estimate, plus the saved markup. The initial markup is 50% when no prior setting exists. Administrators can edit planning rates and markup for future prices. The total shown for your approval at checkout is the fixed payment amount; later provider costs or pricing changes do not increase a completed purchase. Review a changed or expired quote before paying. Payment confirmation alone does not mean a film has been produced.

The owner manages administrator invitations and access. Administrators can review account information and opted-in cloud films, manage customer account status, and maintain pricing settings. Administrator access does not establish that a provider connection, payment, or refund succeeded.
