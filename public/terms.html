Using Lineage Theatre

Lineage Theatre is an invitation-only studio for creating films from family photographs, records, memories, and recordings. Use materials you have permission to use and respect the privacy of living relatives.

AI story suggestions and cinematic scenes are creative proposals. Review the historical evidence, names, dates, relationships, and any dramatization before creating or sharing a film. Generated content is not genealogical proof.

Archive films are composed in your browser. Keep that tab visible during export and download a copy of the finished film. Browser data can be removed by clearing site data or changing devices. Keep your original materials separately.

Third-party studios have their own accounts, pricing, credits, availability, and terms. In-app generated shots use the connected provider account and require confirmation. Lineage Theatre does not currently sell subscriptions or collect payment information.

Read our privacy information or contact admin@brocotech.ai for help.
