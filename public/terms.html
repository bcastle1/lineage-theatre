Using Lineage Theatre

Lineage Theatre accepts public account registration for developing films from family photographs, records, memories, and recordings. Registration creates a customer account. Keep your password private, use materials you have permission to use, and respect the privacy of living relatives.

AI story suggestions and cinematic scenes are creative proposals. Review the historical evidence, names, dates, relationships, and any dramatization before creating or sharing a film. Generated content is not genealogical proof.

Working projects and original sources are saved in your browser. Keep your original materials and download project backups and finished films. You may separately opt to save film details and a selected finished video to the private cloud archive. That archive is accessible to you and authorized administrators. It does not automatically back up your source documents or full working project. Clearing browser data does not remove your account or cloud archive.

GPT-6 Astra is selected for story development, MagicLight for video generation within Lineage Theatre, and QuickBooks for payments. These generation and merchant connections are pending. Film production, customer charges, and refunds are not active. No complete-film price is available until MagicLight supplies a credit quote for the selected quality and production settings.

The default customer markup over MagicLight's cost is 0%. Authorized administrators can set a percentage markup for future quotes. A reference credit rate is not a film total or an approved purchase. Before any future charge, the app must show a confirmed total for your approval. Changing the pricing setting does not alter an earlier completed purchase.

The owner manages administrator invitations and access. Administrators can review account information and opted-in cloud films, manage customer account status, and maintain pricing settings. Administrator access does not establish that a provider connection, payment, or refund succeeded.
