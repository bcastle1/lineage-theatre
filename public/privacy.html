Privacy at Lineage Theatre

You can create an account with your name, email address, password, and acceptance of the terms, privacy information and Source Materials Ownership and Use Agreement. For new registrations we retain the accepted source agreement text, version, content hash, acceptance time and your entered name and account email as the electronic-signature record. New accounts receive customer access; registration does not verify your email address or grant administrator access. Account details, salted password hashes, and consent records are kept in private Vercel Blob storage. Session cookies are secure, HTTP-only, and expire. Accounts issued temporary passwords must replace them before using the studio.

Source ownership, reuse and third-party sharing

New customers must read and expressly accept the Source Materials Ownership and Use Agreement before registration. It transfers the source rights they own or have authority to transfer and authorizes reuse beyond their requested film, including commercial publication, promotion, adaptation, licensing and sharing with third-party companies. These may include service providers, AI and media-production companies, publishers, distributors, commercial licensees, business partners and successors, including outside the United States. Read the full accepted version for its scope and limitations.

Private storage and account controls do not cancel the rights expressly granted in that agreement. Submitted material may be used or published as authorized; do not submit it on the assumption that it must remain confidential. We do not acquire ownership of a person's identity or waive mandatory personal-data rights. Account passwords and payment credentials are excluded from the source-ownership grant. Additional consent and legal requirements continue to apply where required.

This agreement is prospective. Existing customers and earlier materials retain the terms under which they were supplied unless separate agreement is obtained. Publishing an updated statement does not retroactively change an earlier acceptance or authorize broader sharing of earlier submissions.

Protection against automated abuse

Sign-in, account creation, and payment submission use Google reCAPTCHA to detect automated activity. When you submit these actions, Google's script processes browser and device signals under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Our server checks the resulting token before allowing the action. We do not send your password, family materials, or payment card details to the reCAPTCHA verification endpoint. A short-lived payment verification record links your account and quote; reCAPTCHA response tokens are not retained in that record.

Your family archive

New source uploads are saved in your private media library in Vercel Blob storage, with access for your account and authorized administrators. They are available when you sign in on another device. Archiving media or moving it to your trash does not remove the administrator's copy. Administrators have a separate archive and can permanently delete the stored original from it. Previously downloaded copies are not erased. Working project drafts and local source copies remain in this browser; drafts do not automatically sync to another device. Earlier browser-only sources can be copied to the media library using Copy browser sources. Files no longer present in that browser must be supplied again. Someone with access to this browser profile or device may access its local files.

Saving a finished film to the private cloud archive is optional and requires a separate confirmation. It saves the film title, ancestor name, duration, account reference, and the finished video you choose to upload in private Vercel Blob storage. The cloud archive does not upload your source documents or full working screenplay. You and authorized Lineage Theatre administrators can view the saved film details and video. Other customers cannot access them. You can sign in on another device to view films that you have saved to this archive.

Text, PDF, and DOCX files are read locally. Legacy DOC files under 2.5 MB are sent to this app's authenticated server for text extraction. Uploaded originals are retained separately in the media library. Scanned PDFs may need a transcription.

AI assistance and film production

Story development uses OpenAI. Your explicit AI consent and a story request authorize sending the family narrative, extracted text, context notes, and selected reference photos to OpenAI. Source files without readable text, supplied context, or an included reference photo cannot contribute their unseen details. OpenAI's processing and retention terms apply to the materials it receives.

MagicLight is the selected video-generation provider for production within Lineage Theatre. Its film-generation connection is pending; source material is not currently submitted for a MagicLight production. A connected production flow must identify the material being sent and obtain your confirmation before submission.

Administration and payments

The service owner can delegate administrator access through private, expiring invitations. Authorized administrators can review customer account information, uploaded source media, opted-in cloud films, saved pricing settings, and any recorded payment history. Administrative changes are recorded in a private activity log. Password hashes and server credentials are not included in administrator account listings.

QuickBooks is selected for payments. When checkout becomes available, payment processing will be provided by Intuit Payments Inc. The service owner can manage the business authorization in Administration; it is separate from customer accounts and does not activate checkout. If authorized, Intuit access and refresh tokens are encrypted in private server storage. Administrators can view connection status but cannot retrieve those tokens. QuickBooks data is not sent to the film-generation providers or used to train AI. Checkout availability is shown in the studio. A verified production quote or planning estimate plus the saved markup determines the film price. The initial markup is 50% if no earlier setting exists. A displayed checkout total and your approval are required before a charge, and a completed purchase keeps that fixed amount.
