Privacy at Lineage Theatre

Sign-in is available to invited accounts. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes in private Vercel storage. Session cookies are secure, HTTP-only, and expire. Temporary passwords must be replaced before the studio can be used.

Your family archive

Project details, original source files, and exported films are saved in this browser. Projects are separated by the signed-in account. They do not automatically sync to another browser or device. Download your finished film and a project backup, and keep your original source files. Someone with access to this browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored files.

Text, PDF, and DOCX files are read locally. Legacy DOC files under 2.5 MB are sent to this app's authenticated server for text extraction and are not retained there. Scanned PDFs may need a transcription.

AI assistance and rendering

When you enable AI assistance and request story directions or a scene plan, your family story, extracted document text, and source captions are sent to Google Gemini. When you confirm a cinematic take, its visual direction and selected reference image are sent to the chosen video provider. Provider job references and status are held in private Vercel storage under your account so a render can be tracked without starting it again.

Archive-film exports are created in the browser. External studio links do not automatically transfer your sources. If you upload materials there, that studio's privacy and retention terms apply.

Account help

Contact admin@brocotech.ai for account access, password reset, or deletion requests. Clearing this site's browser data removes locally saved projects, sources, and films. Download anything you want to retain first.
