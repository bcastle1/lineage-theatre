Privacy at Lineage Theatre

You can create an account with your name, email address, password, and acceptance of the terms and privacy information. New accounts receive customer access; registration does not verify your email address or grant administrator access. Account details, salted password hashes, and consent records are kept in private Vercel Blob storage. Session cookies are secure, HTTP-only, and expire. Accounts issued temporary passwords must replace them before using the studio.

Your family archive

Working project details and original source files stay in this browser. Projects are separated by the signed-in account and do not automatically sync to another device. Keep your original sources and download project backups. Someone with access to this browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored files.

Saving a finished film to the private cloud archive is optional and requires a separate confirmation. It saves the film title, ancestor name, duration, account reference, and the finished video you choose to upload in private Vercel Blob storage. The cloud archive does not upload your source documents or full working screenplay. You and authorized Lineage Theatre administrators can view the saved film details and video. Other customers cannot access them. You can sign in on another device to view films that you have saved to this archive.

Text, PDF, and DOCX files are read locally. Legacy DOC files under 2.5 MB are sent to this app's authenticated server for text extraction and are not retained there. Scanned PDFs may need a transcription.

AI assistance and film production

GPT-6 Astra story development through OpenAI and MagicLight film production are awaiting verified app connections. When story development becomes available, your explicit AI consent and a story request authorize sending the family narrative, extracted text, context notes, and selected reference photos to OpenAI. Source files without readable text, supplied context, or an included reference photo cannot contribute their unseen details. OpenAI's processing and retention terms apply to the materials it receives.

MagicLight is the selected video-generation provider for production within Lineage Theatre. Its film-generation connection is pending; source material is not currently submitted for a MagicLight production. A connected production flow must identify the material being sent and obtain your confirmation before submission.

Administration and payments

The service owner can delegate administrator access through private, expiring invitations. Authorized administrators can review customer account information, opted-in cloud films, saved pricing settings, and any recorded payment history. Administrative changes are recorded in a private activity log. Password hashes and server credentials are not included in administrator account listings.

QuickBooks is selected for payments. When checkout becomes available, payment processing will be provided by Intuit Payments Inc. The service owner can manage the business authorization in Administration; it is separate from customer accounts and does not activate checkout. If authorized, Intuit access and refresh tokens are encrypted in private server storage. Administrators can view connection status but cannot retrieve those tokens. QuickBooks data is not sent to the film-generation providers or used to train AI. Customer charges and refunds are not active. The default markup over MagicLight's cost is 0%; administrators can change the percentage for future quotes. A complete-film quote and your approval are required before a future charge.

Account help

Contact admin@brocotech.ai for account access, password reset, or deletion requests. Clearing this site's browser data removes locally saved projects, sources, and films; it does not delete account records or films you opted to save in the cloud. Contact the administrator to request deletion of those records. Download anything you want to retain first.
