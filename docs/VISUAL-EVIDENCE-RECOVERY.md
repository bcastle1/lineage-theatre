# Expanded evidence after an inconclusive visual review

The owner can authorize one additional visual review of an existing four-clip Seedance film when its stage-aware v2 review was conclusively recorded as insufficient visible evidence. No dimension may contain a mismatch. This recovery preserves the original manifest, clips, payment coverage and rejected receipts. It creates no video requests, invoice or credit purchase.

The private operator plan contains exactly `email`, `id`, `manifestHash`, `jobHash`, `queueHash`, `priorBindingHash` and `priorReceiptHash`. All hashes come from the current stopped records. `node scripts/recover-film-visual-evidence.mjs plan <private-plan>` validates without changing state. Applying the same reviewed plan uses `apply <private-plan> --apply-reviewed-evidence-recovery`, obtains a fresh payment authorization from the web host, saves an immutable grant, and conditionally resumes only the attention job and queue.

The targeted shot receives nine predetermined samples at 5%, 15%, 25%, 35%, 50%, 65%, 75%, 85% and 95%. The original three sample hashes must still match the prior review. A preceding same-scene sample remains at 85%. The other clips keep their existing review policy. An immutable per-shot claim permits one new inference; an ambiguous response or unsuccessful review cannot reopen that allowance.

Reference availability is determined from actual character photo bindings. A creative description mentioning a labeled portrait does not supply that portrait. Characters with no bound photo are checked against their descriptive traits and visible consistency across the samples, without claiming photographic likeness. Cast, clothing, physical action and image checks still apply. Insufficient evidence is reported separately from a visible mismatch, and both stop finishing.

Deploy the web validator and matching worker together before applying a plan. Delivery independently verifies the v3 grant, both prior decisions, exact source outputs, sample binding, saved passing decision and all other shots. Normal audio, assembly and stored-media checks remain mandatory. Tests use synthetic stories, JPEGs, orders and in-memory records; they do not demonstrate a completed live film.

## Editorial revision after an inconclusive opening action

If the one v3 review still finds only the opening action unverifiable while appearance, wardrobe, cast and image integrity are consistent, an owner may approve one explicit editorial action revision. This changes the requested action; it does not claim that the superseded action was observed. The immutable v4 grant records the original and revised clause, effective visual direction, exact prior v3 decision and frames, original payment coverage, and all four unchanged clip outputs. Narration, dialogue, cast, source material, assembly overlays and existing clip-text corrections remain unchanged.

The private v4 plan uses the same seven fields as v3 plus `originalAction` and `revisedAction`. The old clause must occur exactly once in the opening visual, and its replacement cannot change assembly overlays. `node scripts/recover-film-visual-editorial.mjs plan <private-plan>` is read-only; `apply <private-plan> --apply-reviewed-editorial-recovery` applies the reviewed plan after fresh authorization.

The opening shot receives one genuine review against the revised direction with the same nine frame hashes. The other three shots receive their initial review using nine predetermined frames and their unchanged direction. Approval requires the exact original review inventory and no earlier review claims for those later shots. Each shot has a single immutable v4 claim; uncertainty or an unsuccessful decision never authorizes another attempt. The delivered visual-review proof explicitly retains the approved opening editorial revision. Every shot must still pass the normal checks before assembly and delivery.

## Final-scene editorial revision with three passing reviews

One separately approved v5 revision can follow v4 when its first three shot reviews passed and only the last shot has a scene-action mismatch, with all other dimensions consistent. The exact original and revised action are recorded as a changed editorial requirement. Every v4 claim and receipt, earlier recovery grant, clip output and payment binding is revalidated and retained. This is not an automatic retry or an assertion that the original action occurred.

Use the same nine-field private plan with `node scripts/recover-film-visual-final-editorial.mjs plan <private-plan>`. Applying requires `apply <private-plan> --apply-reviewed-final-editorial-recovery`. The v5 grant allows one new review of the final clip against the revised direction using its identical nine frame hashes. The first three v4 passing proofs are reused without inference. A failed or ambiguous v5 result cannot obtain another attempt. Final verification requires the last-shot v5 proof with its explicit editorial revision and the unchanged first three v4 proofs, including the opening revision. Clip timing, text corrections, narration, original payment and all media bytes remain unchanged.
