# One-scene correction with retained clips

This owner-only recovery creates a new immutable film version under the same film ID and original verified payment. It supports exactly four text-only Seedance scenes: three completed clips are retained, and one failed scene receives an explicitly approved visual-only revision. Narration, dialogue, sources, cast and timing remain unchanged. It never edits the original film or invoice.

Prepare a private import request outside the repository. The authenticated owner submits `createPartialProductionCorrection` with the original order ID, deterministic target prepared ID and manifest hash, selected shot ID, approved visual, maximum new cost, three reuse proofs, reviewed billing evidence, reason and confirmation. The public pure helpers build the exact target manifest and identity. Do not put customer stories, provider URLs or billing exports in source control.

Each reuse proof binds the original operation and request, input hash, immutable receipt hash, byte SHA-256 and size. Billing evidence binds the configured provider account, original order and manifest, private export hash, review timestamp and every original or replacement attempt. Every accepted attempt must be terminal; a current zero-dollar balance observation cannot classify a pending request. The only pre-acceptance exception is the separately recorded, reviewed scope rejection with no accepted request ID.

The cost amounts are an owner-reviewed attestation to private request-level billing evidence. The service verifies its account, record bindings and spending limits; it does not fetch billing events or treat an aggregate wallet change as request-level evidence. The owner imports the reviewed file, sees the proposed scene and new-spend limit, and confirms before submission. An uncertain response keeps the exact request for reconciliation.

Approval requires a failed original job, its attention queue without an active lease, fresh matching worker capability, live original-payment verification and the exact existing unreleased funding reservation. The full original execution budget remains intact; a separate single-scene ceiling and recorded prior spend limit the sole new request. Approval retries reverify payment and reservation without another grant or reservation.

Deploy the web route before the matching worker. The worker advertises `partialCorrectionVersion: 1`; older workers cannot authorize a partial target. The adapter requires its saved request identity, verifies retained clip bytes before returning completion, and never substitutes a new request for missing reuse evidence. Assembly verifies those checksums again and performs the normal fresh visual review, native-audio verification and private-artifact checks. Lost submission replies remain uncertain and cannot trigger another request.

Finished playback and download remain covered by the original payment and its normal access controls. Ordinary later funding admission may retire the original hold only after validating the exact completed correction and private media. No failed source record is rewritten as successful, and no hold is released merely because approval or rendering started.

Validation uses synthetic fixtures only. Run `node --test tests/partial-film-correction.test.mjs tests/fal-funding-correction.test.mjs tests/production-worker-handler.test.mjs tests/production-worker.test.mjs tests/admin.test.mjs`. These tests prove code paths and invariants; they do not claim a live render, charge or delivered film.

## Retry after a confirmed provider service failure

An owner can authorize one retry of the unchanged corrected scene after a confirmed downstream HTTP 500 failure. The separate retry journal preserves the original correction grant and failed provider receipt. It binds the exact failed request, input and record hash to one deterministic replacement operation; the original correction alone cannot authorize that operation. The three completed clips remain intact.

The private import for `retryPartialProductionCorrection` contains the correction ID, prepared ID, manifest hash, shot ID, failed operation identity, failed request ID, failed record hash, maximum new cost, reason, billing evidence and `confirmed: false`. It cannot change a prompt or scene design. Importing the file only opens the review; the owner confirms submission separately. An uncertain browser response retains the exact request for reconciliation, not another paid attempt.

Zero-charge evidence explicitly uses `basis: provider-terminal500-nonbilling-policy` and fal's published pricing policy. It is not a claim that a request-level billing row exists. The server independently checks the exact request's terminal status and recognized downstream HTTP 500 result, then verifies the original payment, provider account and existing funding reservation. A wallet balance alone is insufficient. Moderation failures, uncertain submissions and unknown errors do not qualify.

Deploy the web before its matching worker, which advertises `partialCorrectionRetryVersion: 1`. The approved journal permits the service to resume the failed target and attention queue with conditional writes. The failed receipt remains immutable. The adapter may submit only the journal's one replacement operation, with the original input and the approved scene-cost ceiling. Reconciliation or concurrent submissions must reuse the saved identity. A second failure or lost submission response cannot authorize another render.

The recovered film still passes the normal fresh visual, native-audio, assembly and private-delivery checks. Its original payment governs playback and download. No new invoice, additional reservation or automatic credit purchase is part of this retry.

## Approved narration revision after a rejected retry

When the single retry ends in a confirmed moderation rejection, the owner can approve one successor version with revised narration for that scene. This does not identify which content caused the rejection or guarantee that the revision will be accepted. The earlier versions, grants, retry journal and failed receipts remain intact.

The private `createNarrationSuccessorCorrection` import binds the parent correction, new prepared ID and manifest hash, failed request and record, exact narration, reviewed request-level zero-charge billing evidence, maximum new scene cost, reason and `confirmed: false`. The review panel displays the narration and spending ceiling before a separate confirmation. Its only creative edits are the matching screenplay-scene and shot narration fields. The four shot windows, approved visual design, sources and cast remain fixed; normal word-weighted planning is not rerun. All three reused provider inputs and media checksums must still match.

The server independently checks the exact retry's terminal HTTP 422 moderation result and the validated parent history, including the earlier HTTP 500 failure. Zero-charge evidence uses `basis: provider-request-billing-row` and binds the exact rejected request to its reviewed quantity and total. The existing provider-account reservation and live original payment must still cover the sole replacement scene. An aggregate balance cannot establish this evidence.

Only one narration successor may follow the original correction. Durable intent and conditional records keep concurrent approvals and lost replies on the same saved version; an uncertain result requires reconciliation of the identical request. The narration action cannot create another narration successor or reopen the spent retry. The separately approved background-instruction recovery below is the only additional version supported. No additional invoice, funding reservation, credit purchase or automatic second render is authorized.

Deploy the web first, then the matching worker advertising `narrationSuccessorVersion: 1`. Delivery still requires the normal visual, native-audio, assembly and private-artifact checks. The original funding commitment remains held until the exact validated successor has completed with verified private media. Missing, inconsistent or unfinished successor records keep that commitment held. Playback and download use the original paid order's normal access checks.

## Approved scene background instructions after a rejected narration version

An explicitly reviewed context successor may follow a failed narration version after its exact terminal HTTP 422 moderation result and request-level zero charge are verified. The owner imports `createContextSuccessorCorrection` with the failed narration grant, deterministic new preparation and manifest, selected shot, approved `filmContext` and `historicalBoundaries`, failed operation/receipt references, zero-charge billing evidence, maximum scene cost, reason and `confirmed: false`. File import is review only; the owner separately confirms the two instructions and bounded scene render.

The manifest clone adds a versioned `providerContext` only to the selected shot. This replaces only that shot's two provider prompt lines for film context and historical boundaries. Narration, dialogue, visuals, source references, cast, timing and all other fields remain identical. The global screenplay context stays unchanged, which preserves the three retained provider inputs. The adapter verifies that this override belongs to the exact authorized context successor before a new submission; ordinary client plans cannot introduce it as an unreviewed prompt override.

The two failed versions, their grants and receipts remain intact. The original correction can resolve through at most its narration successor and one context successor. Missing, altered or further links fail closed. A failed or uncertain context successor cannot request another retry or successor. Its fresh payment, original reservation, proof, cost limit and three retained byte checks are mandatory; the normal finishing and private-delivery checks still govern completion and playback.

Deploy the web before the worker advertising `sceneContextSuccessorVersion: 1`. The override is a separately approved creative revision, not evidence of what caused the provider's moderation decision or a guarantee of acceptance. No new customer invoice, credit purchase or additional funding reservation is part of this recovery.
